# 验收题库 · 英语（考研英语一的语法、长难句）

每题是一节「实时黑板」课（2～6 块板）。答案都核对过。

## en-rel　非限制性定语从句

(1) My brother, ______ lives in Beijing, is a doctor.（填关系词）
(2) 改错：The book, that I bought yesterday, is very interesting.
（答案：(1) who；(2) that → which）

教到什么程度：说清有逗号和没逗号意思上的区别（补充说明 / 限定是哪一个）；知道逗号后面（非限制性）不用 that；能在新句子里自己选对 who / which。

## en-subj　虚拟语气（和过去事实相反、混合虚拟）

(1) If he ______ (take) my advice, he would not have failed the exam.
(2) If I had studied medicine, I ______ (be) a doctor now.
（答案：(1) had taken；(2) would be）

教到什么程度：说清从句、主句各用什么形式，分得清「和过去相反」「和现在相反」；第 (2) 题能说出为什么主句用 would be 而不是 would have been（条件在过去，结果在现在：混合虚拟）。

## en-agree　主谓一致

(1) Neither of the answers ______ (be) correct.
(2) The number of students in our class ______ (be) increasing.
(3) A number of students ______ (be) absent today.
(4) Either you or he ______ (be) wrong.
（答案：(1) is；(2) is；(3) are；(4) is）

教到什么程度：说清 neither of 做主语用单数；the number of 和 a number of 的区别；either … or 的就近原则。

## en-long　长难句拆解

The idea that people can change their habits overnight, which many self-help books promote, is not supported by research.
（答案：主干 The idea … is not supported by research；谓语 is not supported；that people can change their habits overnight 是 idea 的同位语从句；which many self-help books promote 是修饰 the idea 的非限制性定语从句。意群：The idea / that people can change their habits overnight, / which many self-help books promote, / is not supported by research.）

教到什么程度：先自己找出谓语；拆出主干；分清同位语从句（that 在从句里不作成分）和定语从句（which 在从句里作宾语）；按意群切开、按意群翻译。

## en-nonfin　非谓语作状语

(1) ______ (see) from the top of the hill, the city looks beautiful.
(2) ______ (finish) his homework, he went out to play.
（答案：(1) Seen；(2) Having finished）

教到什么程度：说清非谓语的逻辑主语就是主句主语；主动用 -ing、被动用 -ed；动作先于谓语、要强调先后时用 having done。
